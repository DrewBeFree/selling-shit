Posting workflow

Use the buttons as launchers, not auto-posters.

  1. Click Copy + open post on the draft for the site you want.
  2. Paste the copied title/body into that site's listing form, then drag photos from the photo tray or download/unzip the photo pack.
  3. After the listing is actually submitted, click Mark posted so the card tracks it.

Marketplace APIs are not reliable for personal listings, so this app keeps your accounts safe by preparing the post instead of bot-posting it.
